## Task (verbatim from the issue body)

Our up-front spec borrowed Steve Yegge's Gas Town vocabulary (Mayor / Witness / Deacon) and assigned those names to the **human's** three seats. On re-reading the source, that mapping is partly a hallucination: in Gas Town those are all **agents**, and the human is the **Overseer**. We want to keep the inversion (it's intentional — see below) but make the analogy land for a reader who knows the post.

Source: https://steve-yegge.medium.com/welcome-to-gas-town-4f25ee16dd04

## The core framing we landed on
- **gt (Gas Town) is one-sided:** sling work. GUPP — "if there's work on your hook, you must run it" — is pure execution-side propulsion. (This is the "self-propelling work" idea; the phrase isn't Yegge's but it fits.)
- **hgt is two-sided:** sling work **then take responsibility for the result.** That second half — review and merge-safety kept with a human — is hgt's whole thesis.

## The intentional inversion (keep this)
Gas Town hands dispatch, oversight, and merge-safety to **agents** (Mayor, Witness, Deacon). hgt keeps a **human** in exactly those seats and gives the machine everything else. That inversion is the point — but the docs must state it explicitly so it reads as intentional, not confused.

## What's wrong / loose today
- **Mayor:** our gloss says "what's worth doing, and in what order." In Gas Town the Mayor is the concierge/chief-of-staff agent that *dispatches* work and gets pinged on completion ("file it and sling it"). "What's worth doing" is actually the **Overseer's** (idea-compiler) job. Decision: **own the collapse** — hgt folds Overseer + Mayor into one human seat — and say so.
- **Witness:** Gas Town's Witness keeps agents *un-stuck* (operational babysitting), not "is it correct" (quality review). Re-gloss.
- **Deacon:** Gas Town's Deacon is a patrolling daemon/watchdog ("runs a patrol… in a loop") — a stretch for "is it safe to land." Re-gloss.
- **Polecats / Crew / Refinery / Dogs:** all agents in the source; make sure hgt only borrows what it actually uses.
